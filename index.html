How to use this app

  
What is it for?

    
It splits the cost of a trip between friends when one person pays by card abroad and the others repay in their home currency at the exchange rate the card used (Monzo's daily Mastercard rate), or at a rate they agree. It also handles big costs, like flights, that one person covers up front and the other repays gradually.

    
It keeps two separate ledgers: the Daily split (everyday shared spending) and the Trip Tab (larger deferred costs). Right now it works in pounds (£) and euros (€).

  
Quick start

    
  1. Tap Trips, then start a new trip. Enter a trip name, everyone's names separated by commas, who pays by card, who picks up the Trip Tab and who owes it. Choose a Euro or Pound trip.
    2. 
    
  2. On Daily split, add each shared spend as it happens.
    3. 
    
  3. On a euro trip, enter the Monzo (Mastercard) rate for that day, or an agreed rate.
    4. 
    
  4. The green banner shows what each person owes the card payer.
    5. 
    
  5. Use Trip Tab for big costs someone else paid up front, then log repayments.
    6. 
    
  6. At the end, open Finish trip for a report.

  
Daily split

    
Pick the day, then add each spend with a short description. Every spend is shared equally between all travellers.

    
On a euro trip, the day's total is divided by the number of travellers, then converted to pounds at that day's rate. The banner shows what each person (other than the card payer) owes. Each day keeps its own rate, so later changes never alter earlier days.

    
Wrong entry? Tap the ✕ beside it. Earlier days are listed under All days, with copy buttons.

  
Trip Tab

    
For larger costs that one person pays up front and is repaid later. The person who pays is "the person who picks up the Trip Tab"; the person who repays is "the person who owes it". You choose both when you start the trip.

    
Add each item in £ or €. A euro item converts at the rate showing on the Daily split page at that moment, and keeps that rate. The tab is completely separate from the daily split.

  
Repayments

    
When the tab is complete, tap Finalise tab amount to fix what is owed. Then log each repayment as it happens: bank transfer, cash, or buying a meal or drink (use its £ value). A running balance shows what is left.

    
If you change the tab after finalising, an Update finalised amount button appears.

  
Finish trip and reports

    
Choose the country, then read the full report: every day and spend, the Trip Tab, repayments and totals. Download PDF, Share PDF, Print / Save as PDF and Copy report text are all there; which ones work depends on your device and browser.

  
Euro trips and Pound trips

    
Euro trip: spends are in euros and converted to pounds using Monzo's daily Mastercard rate, or a rate you agree.

    
Pound trip: everything is in pounds, so there is no rate and no conversion.

  
Trips and people

    
You can keep several trips side by side. Use the Trips button to switch, or start another. The names and roles are fixed once a trip starts, so if someone joins midway, start a new trip.

    
Delete this trip (under Finish trip) removes only that trip. Save its PDF first.

  
Your data and privacy

    
Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your trips are stored in this browser on this device, and they stay until you delete them or clear the browser's data. Other devices have their own separate trips, with no automatic syncing.

    
Because of that, export a backup (in Settings above) and save a PDF when a trip ends.

  
Backup and moving data

    
Export all trips saves every trip to a small file. On another device, choose that file under Import, or paste its text, then tap Import trips. Imported trips are added alongside any existing ones. Importing the same file twice adds the trips twice; use Delete this trip to remove extras.

    
The file contains your trip details, so keep it private.

  
Tips

    
  • Log spends as they happen, not at the end of the day.
    • 
    
  • Monzo's rate changes daily, so check it each day, or agree a fixed rate with your friends.
    • 
    
  • Amounts are rounded to the penny for each day.
    • 
    
  • Install it from your browser menu (Install app or Add to Home Screen) to use it offline.
